Common questions

How do I add songs?

On iPhone, open Settings → Sources and connect Dropbox or pick a folder in iCloud Drive or on your iPhone. Then tap + on the player to choose a song.

Google Drive or OneDrive is grayed out in the folder picker

iPhone doesn't let apps pick a folder from Google Drive, OneDrive or Box. Use Dropbox or iCloud Drive for now.

My notes don't appear on my Mac

Both devices must be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive turned on. Sync usually takes a few seconds, sometimes up to a minute.

How many songs are free?

The iPhone app is free for your first 5 songs. A one-time $4.99 unlock removes the limit. The Mac app is free.

I bought the unlock on another device

Open Settings → Purchase, then tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

I deleted a song or a note by mistake

Right after deleting, tap Undo. Whole songs also go to the Deleted list for 30 days, where you can restore them.
